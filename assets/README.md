# Identidade visual

`orkut-digger-logo-v3.png`: logo atual, uma pá magenta com cabo em forma de “o”, criada com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens. Fundo transparente; os ícones em `../icons/` são reduções com filtro Lanczos. As imagens anteriores preservam as propostas anteriores.

## Prompt da versão com pá

Use case: logo-brand. Create one extremely economical logo for Orkut Digger, an academic browser extension for digital archaeology of archived Orkut communities. Subject: a single small digging spade, unmistakably a shovel, set diagonally, with a rounded lowercase-o ring as its handle, a short thick straight shaft, and a broad gently pointed rounded spade blade. The oversized o handle and vivid Orkut magenta visually evoke Orkut's friendly rounded early-web visual identity. Treat the whole tool as ONE connected solid magenta silhouette with ONLY one negative-space hole in the o-shaped handle. Flat single color #ED008C, thick simple proportions, soft rounded corners, relaxed yet scholarly editorial design, strong distinctive small-icon silhouette. No magnifying glass, no people, no networks, no extra circles, no ornamental strokes, no blade details, no highlights, no shadows, no gradients, no textures, no letters or text outside the handle's abstract o. Square composition, centered with moderate safe padding, truly transparent background. One final standalone logo only, no mockups or presentation.

## Prompt da versão 2

Use case: logo-brand. Design a radically minimal, economical logo for Orkut Digger, an academic browser extension for researching archived Orkut communities. One single bold continuous rounded stroke forms a slightly playful lowercase o that extends into a short diagonal magnifying-glass handle. A distinctive optically balanced mark, not a generic detailed illustration. Only ONE flat solid magenta ink, no outlines inside outlines. Mood: relaxed, intelligent, understated, contemporary independent academic publishing. Extremely few visual elements: just the open counter and short handle, no avatars, nodes, orbital circles, highlights, decorations, textures, gradients, shadows, lettering or wordmark. Soft subtly irregular rounded geometry, clean professional edges, recognizable at 16px. Center the single symbol with generous padding on a square genuinely transparent canvas. Deliver one final standalone icon, no presentation sheet, no mockup.

## Prompt da proposta anterior

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished standalone square app icon for Orkut Digger, a Firefox and Chrome extension for historical research that excavates archived Orkut communities and exports topics and replies. A bold minimal magnifying glass integrated with a lowercase rounded o / community-node motif, suggesting discovery of archived conversations. Friendly early-web character, vivid Orkut-inspired magenta with deep violet accents; strong clean silhouette, thick shapes legible at 32 pixels, centered generous safe margins. Flat vector-like graphic, actual transparent background. No text, no letters other than the abstract o shape, no mockup, no gradients, no shadow, no watermark. Single final icon only.
